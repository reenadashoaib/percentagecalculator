About

This is a free percentage calculator for the everyday questions that come up when shopping, budgeting, studying or reading the news: what's 15% of this bill, how much did the price go up, what did this cost before tax.

Each calculator shows the formula it used alongside the answer, so you can check the working or learn to do it yourself.

How it works

Every calculation runs in your browser. The numbers you type aren't sent to us or stored anywhere.

Why there are ads

Ads pay for hosting and keep the calculator free to use. You can read how advertising partners use cookies in our privacy policy.

Found a mistake?

If an answer looks wrong, tell us the numbers you used and we'll check it.
