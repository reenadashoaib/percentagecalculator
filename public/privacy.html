Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

The numbers you type

Calculations happen entirely in your browser. We don't collect, send or store the numbers you enter.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.

To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy settings link shown by the consent message.

Browser storage

We keep a small note in your browser's session storage so that the full-screen ad shows at most once per visit and a closed video ad stays closed. It is cleared when you close the tab.

Server logs

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request data such as IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions can go to hello@example.com.
